## Aggregates published by this project

The aggregate statistics on the `data` branch (counts and shares per sweep, per day and
per temperature band) are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

You may copy, redistribute and adapt them, including commercially, as long as you give
credit. Suggested credit:

> Source: The Wear Report, aggregate observations from London traffic cameras.
> Powered by TfL Open Data. Contains Met Office data.

Please link to the project where possible.

## Upstream sources

The aggregates are derived from third-party sources that have their own terms. Keep
their attribution when you reuse the data:

| Source | Attribution | Terms |
|---|---|---|

| Met Office Weather DataHub | "Contains Met Office data" | Met Office DataHub terms |
